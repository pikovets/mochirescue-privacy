Mochi Rescue — Privacy Policy

Developer: Paracord Studio · Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short: Mochi Rescue is a puzzle game for children and families. We do not ask for names, e-mail addresses, photos, contacts or any other personal information, and there are no chats and no in-app purchases. Game progress stays on the device. The weekly league uses a random player ID made by the game (no name, no e-mail) stored with our game-server provider Unity Gaming Services; other players only see a made-up name like "Captain Bunny" and the avatar picture shown for that player. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as an ad partner, in child-directed mode for every player, and these receive limited technical information to deliver the ads. The game contains no analytics service.

1. Who we are

Mochi Rescue (the "Game") is developed by Paracord Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's data, contact us at paracordstudio7@gmail.com.

2. Children's privacy

  • The Game is made for a general audience that includes children. We treat every player as a child: the protections of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children and Google Play's Families Policy apply to everyone who plays.
  • We never knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no registration, no password, no chat, no friend lists and no way for a player to type, enter or share personal information. The weekly league (section 5) shows other players only a made-up name from a fixed word list and a picture from a fixed set, never anything a player wrote.
  • The Game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions, and does not use the device's advertising ID. It asks for permission to show notifications only when the Notifications switch in its settings is turned on.
  • Links that leave the Game (this policy, the support e-mail, the store rating page) are behind a parental gate that a young child cannot pass.

3. Information stored on the device

The Game saves your progress locally on the device: completed levels and stars, coins, items bought and worn in the in-game shop (paid with in-game coins only), the daily wheel and daily quests, and the sound, vibration and notification settings. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

If a Google Play Games profile is signed in on the device, the Game links the league's player ID (section 5) to that profile, so the same league player is found again after a reinstall or on another device. Signing in is never required; without it the league works with the player ID of this device only. Google handles the sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive the profile's name, e-mail address or picture.

5. Weekly league (online)

  • To place players in weekly groups, the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save by Unity Technologies), which processes the data below for us as a service provider.
  • On first use, Unity Authentication creates a random player ID for the Game on this device. It is not linked to a name, e-mail address, phone number or the device's advertising ID.
  • For each newly won level the Game sends the level number, the stars, the number of throws and the level's target number, the number of baby Mochi rescued, whether a ×2 boost was on, and the time it was won. The server keeps the player's league tier, weekly group, league points, the levels counted that week, the week's result, and the number of the avatar picture.
  • Other players in the same group see only the place, the league points, a made-up name chosen by the player ID from a fixed list of words (for example "Captain Bunny") and the avatar picture. They never see the player ID.
  • The data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), is used only to run the league, and is not sold or used for advertising. Unity processes it under the Unity Privacy Policy. Without an internet connection the Game plays normally; the league simply waits.
  • Notifications (optional). Only while the Notifications switch in the Game's settings is on and Android allows notifications, the Game shows a local reminder when the free daily spin is ready, and asks Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for a push token for this device. The token is sent to the league server with the device's time zone offset and the Game's language, and is used only to send a short notice when another player overtakes the player in the league. Turning the switch off deletes the token on the device and on the server. The notice contains no name and no message from other players. Firebase processes the token under the Firebase privacy information.

6. Game statistics

The Game contains no analytics service. Apart from the league data in section 5, it sends no statistics about how anyone plays.

7. Advertising

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads only, and only when a player taps a clearly marked button (for example, more coins after a level, a second spin of the daily wheel, or replaying a level already passed). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Watching an ad is always optional.
  • Ads are served through Google AdMob, which may fill a request from Unity Ads, a Google Play Families self-certified ad network; no other ad SDK is used. We tell them that every ad request comes from a child. AdMob is set to G-rated ads only, so only contextual (non-personalized) ads suitable for children are requested. No behavioural advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking is permitted for this Game.
  • To deliver ads, measure that they were shown, limit how often they are shown and prevent fraud, the ad SDKs automatically collect and share the technical information listed in section 8 with Google and, when it serves the ad, with Unity.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not use personalized or behavioural advertising.
  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, precise location or contacts.
  • We do not let players write to each other or see anything another player typed.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases.

10. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on the device until the Game is uninstalled or its data is cleared. League data is kept with Unity Gaming Services while the league runs: a player's tier and avatar number stay with the player, and the weekly groups (places and league points) are deleted about four weeks after their week ends. The push token is kept only while notifications are switched on. Parents may ask us at any time to delete the league data of their child's player: write to us and we will delete it within 30 days. Technical data received by Google and Unity for ads is kept only as long as they need it for the purposes above, as described in their policies; we will forward deletion requests to them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to us and we will help; league data is handled with Unity Gaming Services and ad data with Google and Unity.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the store listing's "What's new" notes.
